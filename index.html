About Me

I'm Abdulrahim, a brand designer and electrical engineering student at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, Nigeria, building my way toward a career in AI engineering. My engineering background shapes how I work: I break problems down, test assumptions, and build in stages, and that same precision carries into my design work. I'm driven by the belief that technology should solve real problems for real people, especially across African contexts. I move comfortably between design, code, and AI depending on what a project actually needs.
